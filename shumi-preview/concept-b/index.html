Who we are

We started because the room did not exist yet.

A handful of women began meeting in a front room in Brockton. Word travelled the way it does — a sister, a cousin, a neighbour. SHUMI is what that became: events, programmes, and a standing invitation.

What we do

Three things, and we do them properly.

01

Gatherings

Sunday events a few times a year. Food, conversation, and someone whose job is to introduce you to three people.

02

Sister circles

Small groups that meet monthly. Twelve women, no agenda, and what is said there stays there.

03

Learning

Practical sessions on the things women actually ask us about — money, health, work, papers. Led by women from the community.

How it goes

Coming for the first time.

Nobody arrives knowing everyone. This is what the day looks like from the door.

01

You book, and we say hello

A ticket, then an email from a person — not a system — with what to expect and who to look for on the day.

02

You arrive, and you are walked in

Someone meets you at the door and introduces you to three women before you have taken your coat off.

03

You leave with somewhere to go next

A sister circle, a session, or the next Sunday. Whatever you want, and nothing you don't.
