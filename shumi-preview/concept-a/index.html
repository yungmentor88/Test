Who we are

We started because the room did not exist yet.

A handful of women began meeting in a front room in Brockton. Word travelled the way it does — a sister, a cousin, a neighbour. SHUMI is what that became: events, programmes, and a standing invitation.

What we do

Four things, and we do them properly.

Gatherings

Sunday events a few times a year. Food, conversation, and someone whose job is to introduce you to three people.

Sister circles

Small groups that meet monthly. Twelve women, no agenda, and what is said there stays there.

Learning

Practical sessions on the things women actually ask us about — money, health, work, papers. Led by women from the community.
