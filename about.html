About

About SHUMI

Stronger Together.

SHUMI began the way the best things do — around a table, between women who refused to let each other shrink. Today it's growing into a hub for connection, resources, opportunity, education, events, and support.

Our Mission

No woman navigates life alone

We exist so that every woman — every age, every background — finds sisterhood, practical resources, and the confidence to pursue what she was made for. SHUMI creates the spaces; the women create the magic.

The vision: a thriving network of women pouring into women, opening doors for one another, and building a legacy of strength for the next generation.

What Guides Us

Our values

01

Sisterhood First

Relationships before programs. Every gathering is designed so real connection can happen.

02

Open Doors

Accessibility in everything — clear information, welcoming spaces, no woman turned away.

03

Growth Together

Education, mentorship, and honest conversation that move women forward in every season.

Our Impact

Small beginnings. Lasting change.

From living-room circles to a growing calendar of events, SHUMI has already touched hundreds of women — and we're just getting started. Every story shared, every resource passed along, every ticket to an event is a seed planted.
