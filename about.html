About SHUMI

Born from a simple belief: women are stronger together

SHUMI began the way the best things do — around a table, between women who refused to let each other shrink. Today it is growing into a hub for connection, resources, opportunity, education, events, and support.

Our Mission

To empower every woman who walks through our doors

We exist so that no woman has to navigate life alone. SHUMI creates spaces — physical and digital — where women of every age and background find sisterhood, practical resources, and the confidence to pursue what they were made for.

Our vision is a thriving network: women pouring into women, opening doors for one another, and building a legacy of strength for the next generation.

What Guides Us

Our values

i.

Sisterhood First

Relationships before programs. Every gathering is designed so real connection can happen.

ii.

Open Doors

Accessibility in everything — clear information, welcoming spaces, and no woman turned away.

iii.

Growth Together

Education, mentorship, and honest conversation that move women forward in every season.

Our Impact

Small beginnings, lasting change

From living-room circles to a growing calendar of events, SHUMI has already touched hundreds of women — and we're just getting started. Every story shared, every resource passed along, every ticket to an event is a seed planted.
