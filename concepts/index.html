The three directions

These are not one design in three palettes. They are three different answers to the question of what SHUMI is for.

CONCEPT 01

Global Editorial

SHUMI as a publication with a point of view. Credibility bought through craft rather than through statistics — which matters, because SHUMI has no statistics yet.

Best if the priority is credibility with sponsors, partners and press.

CONCEPT 02

Human Community

SHUMI as a room you are welcome in. Every decision optimises for belonging over admiration — the largest type, the plainest words, the best mobile.

Best if the priority is filling the 11 October event and growing real membership.

CONCEPT 03

Global Movement

SHUMI as infrastructure for a movement. Dark-first, geography built into the architecture. The only concept where SHUMI’s real pink passes AA as text.

Best if SHUMI genuinely operates in several countries now and intends to build a platform.

Recommended — the SHUMI Hybrid

A blend of three concepts is usually mush. This one is held together by a single rule that resolves every conflict between them.

Warm and light where the site is about people. Dark and editorial where it is about scale.

Warmth for the human sections. Dark, typographically confident treatment reserved for the two moments that need to say this is bigger than one room — the 11 October event and global presence. The contrast between the two modes does work that neither the all-warm nor the all-dark concept could do alone.

Homepage order

  1. Event bar — warm, persistent, ticket CTA
  2. Header — six plain-word items + Contact + ticket
  3. Hero — wide warm photograph, type below, sentence case
  4. Manifesto — one large typeset sentence
  5. The 11 October event — dark full-bleed band
  6. What we do — six blocks, unequal weight, real names
  7. One woman’s story — told at length
  8. Where SHUMI is — dark band, country list
  9. Stories — country-tagged, first at double width
  10. Impact — typeset sentences, empty until real
  11. Get involved — three blocks + reserved growth slot
  12. Partners — names until logos are earned
  13. Newsletter — warm panel, one field
  14. Footer — warm dark, full IA, region slot reserved

Before anything is built

Four things block, and the first one blocks everything.

1. Is SHUMI global, or a Cape Verdean-American organisation in Brockton? The earlier brief said one thing and this one says another. Every decision here rests on the answer.
2. The logo artwork Every colour in this document is estimated from a screenshot.
3. Which countries does SHUMI operate in today? Decides whether the presence section is honest or a claim.
4. Eventbrite link, venue, address, time, price The event page cannot ship without them. It is five weeks out.
