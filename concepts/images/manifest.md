## Concept 01 — Global Editorial

Language: single subjects, hard directional daylight, deep shadow, rich saturated colour,
generous negative space designed in for type. Subjects composed and often meeting the
lens with authority. Global range carried by photographing individual women with equal
seriousness rather than by group shots.

## Concept 02 — Human Community

Language: groups and pairs, soft diffused daylight, warm neutral grade, gentle grain,
shallow depth of field. Subjects mid-conversation, caught rather than posed. Global range
carried by who is in the frame together — many nationalities and several generations at
once.

## Concept 03 — Global Movement

Language: documentary photojournalism. Wide gathering shots with real motion, women at
work in real environments, speakers mid-gesture. Slightly cool contemporary grade,
available light. Global range carried by context — what women are doing and where.

**The rule that governs this set: women as agents, never as recipients.** Nobody in any
frame is being helped. Everyone is working, leading, arguing or building. Photojournalism
of women across countries slides into white-saviour imagery with almost no effort, and
this is the guardrail against it.

## Generation notes worth keeping

Three images needed regenerating after side-by-side review, and one failure is worth
recording because it will recur:

**Naming a medium summons the medium.** Prompts describing "editorial magazine
photography for an international publication" produced photographs *of magazine spreads* —
printed pages with fake body text, page gutters and paper edges. Adding negatives
("no magazine, no printed page, no border") made it worse and produced photographs of
open books, because naming a thing in a negative still puts it in the prompt.

**The fix was to remove every word in the semantic neighbourhood of publishing** — no
"editorial", no "magazine", no "publication", no "medium format film" — and describe only
the scene: a woman, a room, the light, where she stands. That produced clean full-bleed
photographs immediately.

Also regenerated: one Concept 2 group where five women read as too similar in age and
skin tone, replaced with a prompt naming five specific nationalities, ages and hair types.
