Why we exist

Nobody hands a woman a seat at the table. So we brought chairs.

We started because the room did not exist yet. A handful of women met in a front room in Brockton, and word travelled the way it does — a sister, a cousin, a neighbour. Everything since has been built the same way.

Who we are

Three generations, one room, every month.

Grandmothers, daughters and the women who came alone and stayed. SHUMI runs gatherings, small circles and practical sessions — and it holds the door open between them.

What we do

Three things, and we do them properly.

0Women at our tables
0Gatherings held
0Programmes running
01

Gatherings

Sunday events a few times a year. Food, conversation, and someone whose job is to introduce you to three people.

02

Sister circles

Small groups that meet monthly. Twelve women, no agenda, and what is said there stays there.

03

Learning

Practical sessions on the things women actually ask us about — money, health, work, papers. Led by women from the community.
