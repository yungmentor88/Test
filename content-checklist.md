# SHUMI website — what we need from you

**For Melanie, Gabriella and the SHUMI team.**
Prepared by 9ja LDA · 4 September 2026

This is the list of everything the website needs from SHUMI before it can go live. It is
written to be worked through a section at a time — you do not need to do it in order, and
you do not need to do it all at once.

Three things to know before you start:

1. **Nothing on the wireframe has been made up.** Where we did not have a real fact, you
   will see a marked grey box like `[client to supply]` instead of a placeholder number or
   an invented quotation. Those boxes are the items on this list.
2. **Every photograph on the wireframe is a stand-in.** They were generated to show the
   style of photography we are recommending. None of them are real women and none of them
   can go live. They are all replaced after 11 October.
3. **Some items block the October event page.** Those are marked 🔴 and they are the ones
   to do first.

**How to send it:** [9ja LDA to confirm — shared folder or email]

**What makes a good photograph for this site:** women in real proximity to each other,
talking and listening; different generations in the same picture; natural light; the
rooms this community actually uses. What to avoid: everyone lined up smiling at the
camera, and anything that looks like a stock photo.

**Photographer credits:** if a professional took the photographs, we need their name and
whether they want a credit — item 52.

## One decision we need from Melanie

**Should Kriolu appear on the website?**

Our recommendation is yes, but only in three fixed places: the welcome on the homepage
(*Bem-vindu*), the mailing list heading, and the sign-off in the footer. Everything a
visitor has to *use* — every button, every form, every label — stays in English, so a
granddaughter who does not read Kriolu never gets stuck.

You have three options:

- **Yes, as recommended** — three places, everything else English.
- **No** — those three slots take English instead. Nothing else changes.
- **More than that** — then we should talk properly about a bilingual site, rather than
  sprinkling a few words around.

If you choose yes, we also need item 53: **the correct spelling of the Kriolu phrases**,
checked by someone who writes it, not just speaks it.
