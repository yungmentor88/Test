## Voice

Short. Confident. Intelligent. Human. Editorial.

**Banned outright:** leveraging synergies · innovative solutions · cutting-edge
technology · seamless digital transformation · empowering women to unlock their
potential · any sentence that would survive being pasted onto another organisation's site.

**The test every line has to pass:** could this sentence appear on a competitor's website
unchanged? If yes, it is filler and it comes out.

## Headline directions explored for the hero

Recorded so SHUMI can choose rather than receive.

| Direction | Line | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Consequence | *When women move forward, the world moves with them.* | **Used.** States impact without a statistic. |
| Lens | *The world through her.* | Used as the organising idea beneath it. Most ownable. |
| Scale | *One woman. Then everything around her.* | Best sets up the impact → story mechanism. |
| Plain | *A global community for women.* | Safest, least memorable. The baseline to beat. |

## Content rules applied throughout

1. **No invented SHUMI facts.** No statistics, countries, programmes, partnerships,
   testimonials, awards, funding, locations, team members or impact numbers.
2. **No fabricated quotations.** Any quote must be something a named woman actually said,
   used with her permission.
3. **No claimed integrations.** Eventbrite is described as architected for, not built.
4. **AI imagery is labelled as design exploration**, never as documentary evidence of
   SHUMI's work.
5. **Every figure is a placeholder that looks deliberate** — a designed empty state, not a
   blank that reads as an oversight.

## One thing the presenter should be ready for

The likeliest hard question is: *"Why are there no numbers?"*

The answer is on slide 17 and it is worth rehearsing: **because SHUMI does not have
audited numbers yet, and a soft figure on a website is a liability rather than an asset.**
The component is built. The moment SHUMI has something true, it takes ten minutes to turn
on. Until then the story does more work than a number would.
